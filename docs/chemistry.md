# Chemistry

Choose organic, physical or experimental/analytical work below. The branches overlap; select the measurement or chemical claim that matters.

This is a practical starting path. Confirm the methods, local requirements and
current venue instructions for your actual study; this is not an exhaustive standard.

## Choose your route

- Organic: distinguish synthesis/identity/purity, a yield comparison and evidence for a proposed mechanism.
- Physical: decide between measuring a relationship, estimating a rate/thermodynamic parameter and studying a theoretical model.
- Experimental/analytical: define the measurand, matrix and intended use before calibrating or comparing a method.

## Records you need

- Chemical/sample identity, purity or concentration basis, batch, storage and relevant preparation conditions.
- Apparatus and calibration, raw instrument files, processing steps and independent preparations.
- Actual procedures and deviations, appropriate laboratory risk review, waste handling and required trained supervision.

## Study flow

1. State the chemical claim and inspect existing evidence.
2. Define controls, independent preparations and the measurement/analysis model.
3. Plan and authorize laboratory procedures locally; this guide is not an executable lab protocol.
4. Collect raw characterization or measurement records with identifiers.
5. Check model assumptions and alternate explanations; report limits and reproducible methods.

## Analysis checks

- Fit measured responses with an appropriate error model; inspect residuals and calibration range.
- A fitted exponential does not by itself prove a reaction mechanism. Distinguish assumed model behavior from measured kinetics.

## Reporting and formatting

- Define symbols, concentrations, units and uncertainty; connect every plot or spectrum to a sample.
- Report enough characterization and procedure information to assess the stated chemical claim. Follow the target journal for spectra, nomenclature and supporting-information layout.

## Organic chemistry

Record the reaction identity, actual conditions, isolation/quantification basis and independent preparations. Assess identity and purity with suitable characterization. Report unsuccessful attempts that affect the conclusion; yield alone cannot establish mechanism.

## Physical chemistry

Define state variables, equilibrium or kinetic assumptions, temperature control and the observable. Compare plausible models, fit parameters with uncertainty and examine residuals; document computational convergence when modelling.

## Experimental and analytical chemistry

Specify matrix, blanks, reference materials, calibration interval, recovery, precision and interference checks appropriate to intended use. Separate detection from quantification and document validation limits. Do not extrapolate a calibration beyond evidence.

## What counts as done

No synthesis, characterization or laboratory observation is supplied by selecting a template. The example is an unexecuted kinetics plan linked to a mathematical ODE demonstration.
