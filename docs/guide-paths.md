# Research guide paths

Choose a subject path and then a paper path. A paper type is not a discipline.

- [Medical and health](medical.md): Start with the patient, population or biological question, then determine the claim that your design can support.
- [Engineering](engineering.md): Separate a proposed artifact, a controlled performance test and evidence that it works in its intended setting.
- [Materials science](materials.md): Explain how processing and structure relate to the property you intend to measure.
- [Chemistry](chemistry.md): Choose organic, physical or experimental/analytical work below. The branches overlap; select the measurement or chemical claim that matters.
- [Experimental physics](physics.md): Turn the physical hypothesis into a measurable quantity and a traceable measurement model.
- [Theoretical research](theory.md): Theoretical work is an approach across disciplines. Choose the subject area in Plan and define whether you need a proof, a model or a numerical calculation.
- [Research papers](research-papers.md): Write from completed records. A paper is a report of work; selecting a format does not conduct the study.
- [Review papers](review-papers.md): A review is a study of existing evidence. Its search, selection and synthesis are part of the method.
