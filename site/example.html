REAL OBSERVATIONS · EXECUTED CODE

A small study, with its working shown.

How much did NOAA Mauna Loa's equal-weight mean monthly CO₂ concentration change from 2024 to 2025?

Retrospective descriptive reanalysis · Prepared 9 September 2026 · No original data collection or independent review claimed.

Follow the evidenceQuestion & designData & provenanceRun the calculationResult & implicationUse the study record

1. Define an answerable question

This comparison describes a published station record across two selected complete years. It does not ask what caused the change. The unit is a station-month summary, with 12 months per year.

Because the observations already existed and were available during preparation, this is a retrospective reanalysis. We do not present it as a preregistered test or a novel climate discovery.

Read the actual protocol →

2. Inspect the original observations

The input contains 24 unchanged monthly rows from the NOAA CSV retrieved on 9 September 2026, with its explanatory header. The source records month-centering, missing-value flags and station history. The selected period starts after the noted 2022–2023 temporary station substitution.

Data provided by NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory, Boulder, Colorado, USA. The observations are public-domain material under NOAA's terms. This reanalysis is not endorsed by NOAA.

3. Run and verify the calculation

The code requires 12 unique months in each year and rejects invalid, missing, interpolated or nonfinite values. It averages the published monthly means with equal weights, then subtracts 2024 from 2025. It also computes a deseasonalized contrast and the range of same-month differences.

git clone https://github.com/Chandrashekhar-Hegde/open-research.git
cd open-research
python examples/noaa-co2/analyze.py --check
python research.py check examples/noaa-co2 --release

Python 3.11+; no packages or network needed after cloning. The first check compares the computation and input hash with the recorded output. The second checks the study files and evidence references. Neither establishes scientific truth.

4. State the result and its limits

+2.745 ppm

Difference between the equal-weight means of the selected monthly observations.

Quantityppm
2024 equal-month mean424.6042
2025 equal-month mean427.3492
Deseasonalized contrast2.7433

Same-month differences range from 2.09 to 3.85 ppm. The station series increased across this selected period. That is the supported descriptive conclusion.

This is not a global mean or a causal effect. Months are time-dependent, not independent experimental replicates. No significance test or confidence interval is claimed. Equal month weighting differs from day weighting, and future NOAA revisions may change the values.
